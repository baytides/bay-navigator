A PLACE OF MIRACLES
Since 1986, we have been guided by our motto: “compassion, not judgment.” We partner with individuals and families in North Fair Oaks and surrounding communities to provide education, essential services, youth & community programs, and housing to create community connections so that every person can thrive.

40th Anniversary Block Party
Celebrate 40 Years of Compassion, Community & Hope     For 40 years, St. Francis Center has been a place of hope, healing, and belonging for thousands of individuals and families throughout Redwood City. We invite you to celebrate this incredible milestone with us at our 40th Anniversary Block Party, a free community event filled with music, food, family fun, and the opportunity to connect with neighbors while honoring the impact we've made together. Whether you've been part of the St. Francis Center family for decades or are visiting for the first time, we can't wait to welcome you.     EVENT DETAILS   Date: Saturday, September 26, 2026 Gratitude Ceremony: 10:00–11:00 AM at the future site of the Magical Bridge Pocket Park on Buckingham, next to Casa de Sobrato Apartments. As we prepare to begin construction, we gather to thank the organizations and individuals who have championed this special project. All supporters and donors are invited to join us in celebration of our gratitude. 40th Anniversary Block Party Celebration: 11:00 AM–3:00 PM at 151 Buckingham, Redwood City, 94063. Everyone is welcome to attend and enjoy food, music, fun, and special guests.     WHAT TO EXPECT     Bring your family and friends for an afternoon of celebration featuring: Live music and entertainment DJ and dancing Delicious food vendors Activities for kids Ice cream treats Community resource booths Raffles and giveaways Much more! There's something for every age to enjoy as we celebrate four decades of serving our community.

OUR SERVICES
The St. Francis Center in Redwood City offers a comprehensive range of services to support low-income families and individuals in the North Fair Oaks neighborhood. Their programs address immediate needs and promote long-term self-sufficiency.
