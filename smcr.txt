Reduce, Reuse, Repair
Choosing to reduce, reuse, and repair is among the most effective ways to minimize your environmental footprint. These practices help decrease water and air pollution caused by waste and production, conserve valuable resources, and reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills.
Reduce
Minimize waste and conserve resources by avoiding unnecessary purchases and preventing waste at its source.
Buy only what you need
Purchase products in bulk to reduce packaging
Purchase locally produced items
Rent or borrow instead of buying new
Reuse
Extend the life of items by reusing, repurposing, or donating them to keep functional items out of landfills.
Use reusable items (bags, bottles, foodware)
Select durable products over disposable ones
Donate gently used clothes or home goods
Repurpose containers or materials for other uses
Donate or buy salvaged building materials
Repair
Fix broken or damaged items to restore their functionality and extend their lifespan, reducing the need for replacements.
Mend clothing
Repair electronics
Fix damaged furniture
Programs and Resources
Explore our initiatives and tools designed to promote waste reduction and reuse.
Fixit Clinic (Repair)
Fixit Clinics are community-based workshops where volunteer Fixit Coaches help you learn how troubleshoot, maintain, and repair your broken items. Register for an upcoming event and discover the skills to fix your belongings yourself!
Food Waste Prevention
Food waste depletes natural resources, uses valuable land, and significantly contributes to climate change by releasing methane. Discover solutions for preventing food waste.
Surplus Property Program
The Surplus Property Program beneficially reuses surplus equipment from San Mateo County departments. Learn how San Mateo County departments, cities, schools, and non-profit organizations can request retired property at no charge.
Edible Food Recovery
Free hands-on technical assistance to help businesses and organizations comply with SB 1383 edible food recovery requirements, including support setting up food recovery contracts and educating staff on safe food donation handling.
Foodware Aware Program
The Disposable Food Service Ware Ordinance, adopted February 25, 2020,  aims to regulate and reduce the amount of disposable food service ware that is used by food facilities in unincorporated areas of San Mateo County.
Building Materials Reuse
Reusing building materials is a cost-effective and environmentally responsible alternative to recycling them or buying new materials.
Propane Refuel
The ReFuel Your Fun (RFYF) Program aims to minimize the use and improper disposal of single-use 1 lb. propane cylinders by providing discounted propane refills for reusable cylinders.
